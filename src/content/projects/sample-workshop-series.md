Our team will develop an EEG-controlled adaptive lighting system that controls the brightness and color temperature of a study lamp based on the alpha and beta activity in a user’s brain. We will begin by acquiring EEG signals using an EEG acquisition headset and process the signals through a connected OpenBCI board to isolate the relevant frequency bands for alpha and beta activity. Using signal processing techniques, we will extract specific features from these bands to create signals that control the lamp’s brightness and color temperatures. We will then pass these signals from the OpenBCI board to an LED lamp to directly control the brightness and color-temperature. 

We want the first phase of the project to be centered around teaching the relevant topics to the newer members and the second phase to be centered around development. Based on the team lead application that we send out, we can divide our team members into the relevant sections that they want to work on (e.g. Signal Processing, Firmware, etc.). This information will also be used to determine how we assign tasks to the members in each subteam, as we will take into account how experienced the members are in their subteam.
