Synapse's Journal Club gives members the opportunity to explore current research in neuroscience and neurotechnology through student-led presentations and group discussions. Each session focuses on a selected research paper, with members breaking down the background, methods, results, and broader implications of the work. We encourage members to ask questions, challenge conclusions, and think critically about how researchers approach problems in the field.

The Journal Club is open to students of all experience levels, whether they're reading their first neuroscience paper or already involved in research. Through these discussions, members gain experience interpreting scientific literature, communicating complex ideas, and learning about topics outside their own areas of study. We hope to create a space where students can stay up to date with developments in neurotechnology, learn from one another, and discover new research interests.
