## Who can join

Any Georgia Tech student, at any level and in any major. Our workshops start from the basics so you don't need prior experience in neuroscience, programming or electronics.

## What membership looks like

Team members will typically contribute 2-3 hours per week for their project, depending on the project team. Currently, we don't require dues.
