## Our mission

Synapse is a student-led community dedicated to making neurotechnology more accessible through interdisciplinary collaboration and hands-on projects. Neurotechnology is inherently an interdisciplinary field; we bring together students from all backgrounds to explore the intersection of neuroscience and engineering while developing practical skills. 

## How we're organized

Synapse runs three kinds of activities:

- **Engineering Projects**: hands-on teams building EEG, brain-computer interface, and neural-data tools.
- **Journal Club & Research**: members read, present and debate current neuroscience and neurotechnology papers.
- **Workshops & Education**: intro neurotech workshops, Python and signal-processing tutorials, and guest talks.
- **Newsletter/Magazine:** keep up to date with the latest in Synapse, neurotech, and neuroscience.

Members come from EE, BME, neuroscience, CS, psychology, and more. No experience is required!
